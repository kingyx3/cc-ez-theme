## Shipping & Delivery Policy

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

### Where we ship

We currently ship within Singapore only. We do not ship internationally.

### Shipping fees

- Orders below S$500: S$10 flat-rate delivery.
- Orders of S$500 or more: free delivery.
- Orders of S$1,000 or more: free delivery, or a free meet-up at Outram Park MRT by arrangement.

The shipping fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

### Processing and delivery times

- **In-stock items:** orders are typically processed and packed within 0–5 working days after payment is confirmed.
- **Delivery:** once dispatched, delivery usually takes an additional 1–3 working days via SingPost or our delivery partners. Tracking details will be provided once available.
- **Pre-order items:** we only take pre-orders against stock already allocated to us by our distributor. The release date shown on each pre-order page is the manufacturer's expected date, not a guarantee. Release dates, and occasionally allocation quantities, are set by the manufacturer and our suppliers. We have no control over delays, or over a supplier failing to deliver what it committed to. If a release is delayed, we will tell you by email or WhatsApp as soon as we hear.

Pre-orders are typically processed and packed within 0–5 working days after the stock arrives at our facility. If the manufacturer cancels a product, we will refund you in full. All other pre-order sales are final, and a delay is not grounds for a refund. If a delay affects you, contact us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/6589808075) (+65 8980 8075) and we will look at it case by case.

Working days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and Singapore public holidays. Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to courier or other unforeseen delays.

### Orders with mixed items

If an order has both in-stock and pre-order items, we ship them together, unless the in-stock and pre-order items are expected to be ready more than 2 weeks apart. In that case we ship them separately.

### Damaged, wrong or missing items

Please contact us within 3 days of delivery at [contact@cardboard.sg](mailto:contact@cardboard.sg) or on [WhatsApp (+65 8980 8075)](https://wa.me/6589808075). See our [Refund & Returns Policy](https://cardboard.sg/pages/refund-policy) for details.
