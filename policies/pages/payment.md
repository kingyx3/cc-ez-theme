## Payment

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

We take payment securely at checkout through HitPay, a licensed Singapore payment provider. We accept PayNow only.

Your order is confirmed automatically once HitPay confirms the payment. You don't need to send screenshots or make a manual transfer.

Please complete payment within about 15 minutes of checkout. Unpaid orders are cancelled automatically, and the stock is released.

Prices are in Singapore dollars (SGD). The order total, including shipping, is shown before you pay.

We never ask for payment outside checkout, and we will never ask you to pay to a personal account.
